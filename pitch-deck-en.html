The core problem

Data fragmentation is killing onboarding.

30–50% of qualified applicants abandon before onboarding completes

A 24–48 hour approval should be routine. Instead it takes 7–14 days, because the answer is scattered across 4–6 disconnected systems: CRM, KYC platform, document storage, compliance database.

The workflow, as it actually runs

Four stages. Nobody owns the handoff.

Stage 1→Sales and Onboarding hand off by email, with no shared record to check.Sales → Onboarding
Stage 2→Compliance manually cross-checks 4+ systems, ~45 minutes per file.Onboarding → Compliance
Stage 3→Underwriting re-gathers data Compliance already verified.Compliance → Underwriting
Stage 4→IT, Ops, and Compliance sign off separately, with no single source of truth.Underwriting → Setup → Live
Ideal: 12 hours Typical: 12–26 days With issues: up to 67 days
Source: internal cross-functional workflow audit (10-step onboarding process, compressed to 4 stages here).

Why an AI layer, not shared access

Teams query only their own sources, or sources explicitly opened to them. Cocevo's backend AI reads the closed sources and returns only what's compliant to share, never the raw data or the prompt behind it.

Why this matters for compliance

Every team keeps its confidentiality boundary. No blanket access to another department's raw systems, just the answer they're allowed to see.
