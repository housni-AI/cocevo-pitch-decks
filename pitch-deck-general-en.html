Why an AI layer, not shared access

Teams query only their own sources, or sources explicitly opened to them. Cocevo's backend AI reads the closed sources and returns only what's safe to share, never the raw data or the prompt behind it.

Why this matters for trust

Every team keeps its confidentiality boundary. No blanket access to another department's raw systems, just the answer they're allowed to see, wherever internal or regulatory rules apply.
